Quick win 21 of 29 · Say what happens after a valuation

What we're proposing

Keep the three-step valuation form, and put a short panel beside it: the ways Mayfair Prestige can buy or sell a car, how quickly they'll come back, and a separate, private route for anyone selling a collection.

Why

Sell your car is a clear three-step form. What it doesn't say is what happens next, how fast, or whether they buy outright, take a part exchange or sell on commission. The homepage mentions experience in "acquiring or selling collections", but there's no route for it.

Timings and terms in brackets are for Mayfair Prestige to confirm.
