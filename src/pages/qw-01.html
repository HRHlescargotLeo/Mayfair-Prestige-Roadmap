Quick win 1 of 29 · Cars on the homepage

What we're proposing

Add a "Latest arrivals" row under the search band: eight cars, newest first, each with its photo, price, mileage and colour, and a link to the full inventory. It fills itself from the stock, so nobody has to maintain it.

Why

The homepage has a search band, three service tiles and several paragraphs of text. None of the 40 cars in stock appears on it, so a visitor has to search before they see anything for sale.
