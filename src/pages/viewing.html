What we're proposing

A viewing request in four short steps: in the showroom or on a video call, a day and a rough time, your details, and a confirmation that says what happens next. The car you came from is carried through, so nobody types its make and model into a box.

Two companion pages complete the prototype: Reserve, which explains what reserving means before asking for anything, and Contact, which finally has the phone number on it.

Why

Viewing by appointment is the whole model, and the site says so on almost every page. Booking one means choosing "Book an Appointment" from a 13-option list on the contact form, or pressing "Arrange a Viewing" on a car and landing on the Reserve form, which never mentions a viewing.

A request isn't a confirmed booking, and the prototype doesn't pretend otherwise. It asks for a preference and says someone will call to confirm. That keeps the diary in the showroom's hands, which seems right for a business this size.

Days run from tomorrow. Times follow the Location page's hours, which are an open question.
