Quick win 5 of 29 · Cars sooner on phones

What we're proposing

On phones, use a slim photo strip and a single "Filter and sort" button that opens a sheet with all the filters. The first car then appears on the first screen.

Why

At phone width the hero photo and six stacked dropdowns come first. The first car card on the Inventory page starts about 1,178 pixels down, more than a full screen of scrolling before anything for sale.
