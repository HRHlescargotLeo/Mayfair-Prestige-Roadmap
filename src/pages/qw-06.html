Quick win 6 of 29 · Colour and photo count on cards

What we're proposing

Replace "Speed" on stock cards with the colour, and show how many photos the car has. Colour is one of the first things a buyer filters on in their head, and a photo count tells them how much there is to see.

Why

Cards show miles, gearbox, year, fuel and top speed. Colour isn't shown anywhere on the card, and top speed is the least useful of the five for this stock: on the classics it reads "-".

Colours are sample data until the stock feed includes them.
