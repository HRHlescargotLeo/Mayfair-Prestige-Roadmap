Quick win 8 of 29 · Each car its own title and address

What we're proposing

Give every car page its own title ("2014 Ferrari 458 Speciale, 6,500 miles | Mayfair Prestige"), a written description, its own address as the canonical, and a share image. All of it comes from the stock data, so it costs nothing per car.

Why

All 40 car pages have the title and description "Vehicle Detail | Mayfair Prestige" and tell search engines that their real address is /vehicle-detail/. That says the 40 pages are one page, so someone searching for a used Phantom in London is unlikely to find the five Mayfair Prestige has.
