Quick win 28 of 29 · Keep registrations out of links

What we're proposing

Pass a stock number in the reserve and enquiry links instead of the registration, and show the plate only where Mayfair Prestige chooses to.

Why

Reserve links carry the registration in the address, for example /reserve-now?make=Ferrari&model=458®istration=GV64XDZ. Addresses end up in browser histories, analytics and shared links.
