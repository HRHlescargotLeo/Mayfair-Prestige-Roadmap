Quick win 2 of 29 · Let buyers sort the inventory

What we're proposing

A sort control above the results: price high to low (today's order), price low to high, newest arrivals, lowest mileage and newest year. The choice is remembered as the buyer pages through.

Why

Inventory lists 40 cars, twelve a page, always from most to least expensive, with no way to change the order. A buyer looking for a low-mileage car, or for what came in this week, has to read every page.
