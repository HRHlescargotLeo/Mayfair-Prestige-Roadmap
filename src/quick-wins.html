29 quick wins for the Mayfair Prestige website

Smaller changes that can each be made on their own, without any of the five big improvements. Each one has its own page showing just that change, with today's version beside the proposed one where a comparison helps.

How to review

  • "Today" is rebuilt from what we saw on the live site; "Proposed" is the change. Everything else on the page is kept the same, so the difference is the change itself.
  • Use Quick win in the bar at the top to jump to any of them, or the previous and next links at the foot of each page.
  • Switch Notes on to see what each quick win proposes and why, with notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Mayfair Prestige (red).
  • Several quick wins also appear inside the five big improvements. Here each one stands alone, so they can be chosen separately.
