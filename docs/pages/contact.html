Prototype 3 of 5 · Contact

What we're proposing

Put the ways to get in touch at the top: phone, WhatsApp, email, the address, opening hours and directions. Below that, four short forms for the four things people actually get in touch about, and the people they'll be dealing with.

Why

The contact page is a form and nothing else. It opens with a 13-option "Nature of Enquiry" list, and asks for make, model and registration as typed text even when you've just come from a car. The phone number isn't on the page, and no number anywhere on the site can be tapped to call.

The Meet the Team page lists a photographer, a head detailer and a valeter. Daniel, who is named in six of the eight testimonials, isn't on it.
