Quick win 24 of 29 · One set of facts

What we're proposing

Agree each fact once and store it in one place in Umbraco, so the header, footer, contact page and car pages all show the same thing. Changing the hours then means changing them once.

Why

The site gives three different lengths of time trading, three versions of the opening hours (plus AutoTrader's), two phone numbers and two figures for the showroom. Each one is small, but a buyer comparing pages notices.
