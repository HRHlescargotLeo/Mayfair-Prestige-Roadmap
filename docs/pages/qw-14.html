Quick win 14 of 29 · A contact page with the details on it

What we're proposing

Put the phone number, WhatsApp, email, address, hours and a map link at the top of Contact us, then offer four routes (buying, selling, finance, storage), each opening a short form that asks only what's needed.

Why

Contact us is a form and nothing else. The address is only in the footer and there's no phone number. The form asks for a "Nature of Enquiry" from 13 options, then make, model and registration as free text.
