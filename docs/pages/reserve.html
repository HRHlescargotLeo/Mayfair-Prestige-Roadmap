Prototype 3 of 5 · Reserve a car

What we're proposing

Show the car being reserved at the top of the page, explain in three lines what reserving involves, and only then ask for details. The confirmation gives a reference and says who will call. Later, if Mayfair Prestige wants it, the same page could take a refundable holding deposit online.

Why

Today's Reserve page says "Please fill out the following form for a Mayfair Prestige representative to get in touch with you." The make, model and registration travel in hidden fields, so the buyer never sees which car they're reserving, and nothing says whether money changes hands, for how long the car is held or what happens if they change their mind.

Someone about to commit to a six-figure car would reasonably want to know.

The terms in square brackets are placeholders; we don't know Mayfair Prestige's reservation terms. Defaults to the 2020 Rolls-Royce Cullinan if no car is chosen.

How reserving works
