Quick win 25 of 29 · Rewrite the homepage copy

What we're proposing

Rewrite the homepage text in the plainer, more confident voice of the newer About and Location pages, and proofread it.

Why

The homepage has phrases such as "all prepare to our exacting standards", "We are independent company", "from barbs conversions" and "Oldtimer and Youngtimer". The About and Location pages read much better, so the homepage is the odd one out, and it's the page most people see first.

Suggested wording, to be checked against what Mayfair Prestige wants to say.

Try: Tick "Show what changes" to see the phrases that would go.

Today

Phrases on the homepage today:

  • "all prepare to our exacting standards"
  • "a viewing by an appointment policy"
  • "We are independent company"
  • "ultimate best manufactures of bulletproofing vehicles"
  • "from barbs conversions"
  • "all leading motor way within 10 minutes"
  • "Oldtimer and Youngtimer"

Proposed

Mayfair Prestige is an independent specialist in luxury, performance and classic cars, from our showroom on Crawford Street in Marylebone. Every car is inspected and prepared to our own standards before it goes on sale.

Viewings are by appointment, so you have the car and our time to yourself. We're a few minutes from Marylebone and Baker Street stations, and close to the A40.

We also find cars to order, arrange finance, store and deliver cars, and supply armoured vehicles and Brabus and other conversions for private and diplomatic clients.

Open questionShould the homepage keep the long-form text at all, or move most of it to About and keep the homepage for cars and services?
