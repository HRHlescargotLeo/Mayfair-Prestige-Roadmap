Prototype 2 of 5 · Every car, properly presented

What we're proposing

Put the price, mileage, colour, owners and the three next steps beside the photos, where a buyer looks first. Below them, a list of the things a buyer of a £300,000 car will ask about anyway: owners, service history, warranty, where it was supplied, what it was specified with. Then a short description written about this particular car, a video walkaround, a finance figure and cars like it.

Behind the scenes each car gets its own page title, description and structured data, so search engines see 40 cars rather than one page 40 times.

Why

On the live site the 2014 458 Speciale, at £519,950 the most expensive car in stock, has its price under a long general description, which finds room to mention the Bluetooth. Mileage isn't in the Car Details panel. "Arrange a Viewing" and "Reserve Now" go to the same form.

Every car page currently has the title "Vehicle Detail | Mayfair Prestige" and tells search engines its real address is /vehicle-detail/. In practice that means someone searching for a used Phantom in London is unlikely to land on any of the five Mayfair Prestige has.

Open any car from the inventory to load it here; by default it shows the 2014 Ferrari 458 Speciale. Key information, options and the description are sample content.
