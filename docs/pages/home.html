Prototype 1 of 5 · The showroom online · Homepage

What we're proposing

A homepage that shows the cars. Under a slimmer search we'd put the six most recent arrivals, one featured car with a line about why it's worth seeing, and the stock grouped into the collections people actually come for. Reviews, the "By invitation" list and the private services follow, then how to visit.

The inventory is the other half of this prototype.

Why

The current homepage has a search form, three service tiles and several paragraphs of older copy. Not one of the 40 cars in stock appears on it. A visitor who doesn't yet know what they want has nothing to look at until they've chosen a make and pressed a button.

The stock itself is the strongest argument Mayfair Prestige has. Nine Rolls-Royces, six Ferraris and three 1960s Mustangs say more about the business than a paragraph about passion for luxury on four wheels.

Latest arrivals use sample "days in stock" figures. Prices, mileages and derivatives are from the live site.
