Quick win 23 of 29 · Delivery offered on every car

What we're proposing

Add one line to every car page, "Delivery to your door, UK and worldwide", opening a short transport quote: the car, where it's going, road, sea or air, and when.

Why

Transport "in the UK and Europe… worldwide… via air, sea, or road" is mentioned only on the Storage page. A buyer outside London looking at a car has no reason to know it's offered.
