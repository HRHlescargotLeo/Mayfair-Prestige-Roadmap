Prototype 4 of 5 · Previously sold

What we're proposing

Show the sold cars as a gallery with their photos, filter them by marque, search them, and put "find me one like this" on every one, which opens the sourcing brief already filled in.

Why

Previously Sold lists 84 car names, nine to a page over ten pages, in alphabetical order. The names aren't links, and the 84 addresses the sitemap gives for them all return "page not found". Seven Bugatti Veyrons, an Enzo and an F40 are buried on pages four to eight.

As evidence of what Mayfair Prestige can find, it's the best thing on the site. It just isn't presented as such.

Names and order are as on the live site. Years and sale dates aren't published, so the cards don't show them.
