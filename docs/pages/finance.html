Prototype 5 of 5 · Finance

What we're proposing

Explain each finance product in two lines, let people work out an indicative monthly figure for any car in stock, and put the finance specialist's name and number where they can be tapped. The same calculator feeds the "from £x a month" figure on each car page.

Why

The finance page is several paragraphs about "bespoke funding solutions" and "first-rate relationships with leading finance providers". The products are only named on the homepage, and none is explained. The specialist's mobile number is shown as text with a telephone emoji in front of it.

Mayfair Prestige is FCA-authorised as a credit broker, so the page can say a good deal more than it does, provided the wording is signed off.

The calculator uses an illustrative 9.9% APR so the figures move. It is not a quote or a representative example; the real wording and rates need Mayfair Prestige's compliance sign-off.

The options

Hire purchase

Pay a deposit, then fixed monthly payments. The car is yours after the last one.

Lease purchase

Lower monthly payments with a larger final payment at the end, after which the car is yours. Mostly used by businesses.

Contract hire

A fixed monthly rental for an agreed term and mileage. You hand the car back at the end.

Personal contract hire
