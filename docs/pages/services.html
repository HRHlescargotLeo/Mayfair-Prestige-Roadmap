What we're proposing

One page for the things Mayfair Prestige does besides selling cars from stock: armoured vehicles, bespoke work and conversions, storage, delivery and helping buyers from abroad. Each has a short explanation and its own private enquiry, and storage gets a cost calculator built from the price already published.

A finance page with an indicative calculator completes the prototype.

Why

The homepage tile for armoured vehicles links to the general inventory, which has no armoured cars and no way to filter for them, while the copy beneath describes sales to embassies, government officials and royalty. Bespoke work gets a paragraph mentioning "barbs conversions" and nowhere to go.

Storage is the exception: it states the price and the minimum term. It deserves photos and an easier way to ask.

Text in square brackets needs Mayfair Prestige's input. Protection levels are given as the standards buyers will recognise; which ones Mayfair Prestige can supply is an open question.
