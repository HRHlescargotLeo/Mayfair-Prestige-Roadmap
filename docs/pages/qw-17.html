Quick win 17 of 29 · Lighter forms

What we're proposing

Fill in the car the buyer came from, ask only for a name and a way to reply, add an optional message, and make finance an optional tick ("I'd like to talk about finance").

Why

The reserve form requires a yes or no answer about finance and has no message box. Contact asks for the make, model and registration as typed text even when you've come from a car.

Quick win 17

Lighter forms

Ask only what's needed, fill in the car, add a message box and make finance optional.
