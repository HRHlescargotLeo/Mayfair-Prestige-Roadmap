Prototype 1 of 5 · The showroom online · Inventory

What we're proposing

Filters down the side that say how many cars each choice leaves, a sort control, budget bands that reach the whole range, collections as one-tap shortcuts, and cards that show colour and the number of photos instead of top speed. Any car can be saved to a shortlist, and up to three compared side by side. On a phone the filters fold into a single button, so the first car is on the first screen.

Why

Today the list is always most expensive first, with no way to change it. Price is a maximum only, starting at £50,000, so the five cars below that can't be found by budget at all. Mileage is a list of 85 options in 1,000-mile steps.

On a phone the hero photo and six stacked dropdowns come first, and the first car appears about 1,178px down the page. Someone browsing on the way home has given up long before that.

Colours and statuses are sample data. The shortlist and comparison are kept for the visit only.
