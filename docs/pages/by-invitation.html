Prototype 4 of 5 · By invitation

What we're proposing

Turn the footer's "By invitation" sign-up into something a buyer would actually fill in: choose the marques, body styles and budget you care about, see how many cars in stock match today, and hear about new arrivals before they're listed. Next to it, a short "find me a car" brief for the car that isn't in stock.

The Previously Sold page becomes the proof that the sourcing works, with "find me one like this" on every car.

Why

The homepage says Mayfair Prestige can "procure the car you are looking for within a short period of time if it should not be in our inventory". There's nowhere on the site to ask for that; the contact form's 13 enquiry types don't include it.

"By invitation – private updates on current and upcoming availability" is the best line on the site. At the moment it's a single email field in the footer, with no say over what you'll be sent.

The count of matching cars uses today's 40 cars. Marques not currently in stock (Bugatti, McLaren and so on) can still be chosen.
