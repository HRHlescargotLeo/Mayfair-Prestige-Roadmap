Five ideas for the Mayfair Prestige website

Clickable prototypes of five improvements to the Mayfair Prestige website. They use the 40 cars currently in stock, and they work: filters, saving, booking and the calculators all respond as they would on the live site.

The prototypes

  1. The showroom online
    1

    The showroom online

    A homepage that leads with the cars, then an inventory with collections, sorting, budget bands that reach every car, a shortlist and side-by-side comparison.

    Try: open the Rolls-Royce collection, sort by lowest mileage, save two cars and compare them.

    Open prototype
  2. The car page
    2

    Every car, properly presented

    Key facts beside the photos, the car's history, a video walkaround, a finance figure, three clear ways to take the next step, and a page search engines can tell apart from the other 39.

    Try: switch between Exterior and Interior photos, then share the car by WhatsApp.

    Open prototype
  3. Viewing and reserving
    3

    Book a viewing, reserve with confidence

    Ask for a viewing at a time that suits, in the showroom or by video call. Alongside it, a reserve page that explains what reserving means, and a contact page with the details on it.

    Try: book a video call for next Saturday, then open Reserve and read how it works.

    Open prototype
  4. By invitation
    4

    By invitation

    For the buyer who hasn't found it yet: alerts by marque and budget, a "find me a car" brief, and Previously Sold as a gallery of 84 cars, each with "find me one like this".

    Try: pick Ferrari and Rolls-Royce under £250,000 and watch the count, then ask for a car like a sold 812 Superfast.

    Open prototype
  5. Private services
    5

    Private services

    Armoured vehicles, bespoke work, storage, delivery and buying from abroad on one page with private enquiries, plus a finance page with an indicative calculator.

    Try: work out six months' storage, then price the Ghost on lease purchase.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • These are designed to sit with the current Mayfair Prestige site: its typeface, its black, bone and white palette, square corners and the cars' own photography, loaded from mayfairprestigeuk.co.uk.
  • Everything interactive works: filters, sorting, saving, comparing, the gallery, booking steps, forms and calculators. Links to pages outside these five go nowhere by design.
  • There are two tabs at the top. Big improvements holds these five prototypes. Quick wins holds smaller changes that can each be made on their own.
  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes. Switch Notes on (top right) to see what each prototype proposes and why, with notes on behaviour (yellow) and open questions for Mayfair Prestige (red). Notes start switched off.
  • Stock, prices, mileages and derivatives are from the live site. Colours, owners, statuses and service details are sample data and say so. Finance figures use an illustrative rate and are not quotes.
  • Works on a phone: open this page on your mobile to check the layouts.
